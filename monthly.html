Game Plan

Focus For Next Month

1

Convert the 18 trials Priority

July brought a record 18 New Client Specials. That's the warmest pipeline we have and the clearest path to growing the active base past 35. A consistent day-10 check-in and membership offer before each pass expires is the highest-leverage habit this month.

2

Tighten lead response time Improve

Only about 60% of leads get a reply within 24 hours, and many wait longer. We have the volume (224 leads) and tripled real conversations (60), so faster, more consistent Inbox replies convert more of them.

3

Grow the active base, not just sales Watch

We sold plenty but the active recurring count held at 35. Retention and activation - getting new sign-ups to stick and winning back the 11 paused members - is what actually moves us toward 70.

4

Finish the check-in habit Almost there

Attendance marking jumped from 28% to 84% coverage - huge. Get the 12pm class checking people in and we'll have complete, trustworthy attendance across every session.

1

Convert trials to memberships

Target: convert 40%+ (~7 / mo)
2

Hold churn under 6 a month

July lost 6 recurring members, same as June. Personal check-ins with quieter members and a save conversation at cancellation keep people in the gym.

Target: 6 → 3-4 cancellations
3

Answer leads faster

224 leads produced ~18 trials in July (~8%). Only 60% of leads get a reply within a day - tightening that turns more leads into first classes.

Target: 80%+ answered < 24h
4

Win back paused & lapsed

11 memberships are paused and July's trials are expiring now. A friendly "we miss you" message to familiar faces is low effort and often brings them back.

Target: recover 3-4
