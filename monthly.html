Game Plan

Focus For Next Month

1

Convert the 16 trials Priority

Trial-to-member conversion slipped to 23% on the mature cohort (7 of 31). A consistent day-10 check-in and membership offer before each pass expires is still the highest-leverage habit, especially with fewer new leads coming in.

2

Fix the lead drop Watch

New leads fell from 224 to 64 even as website sessions rose to 640. Ads look paused or under-delivering. Every one of those 64 has to get a fast reply: 14 show a chat reply on last activity.

3

Hold cancellations and paused members Watch

True cancellations rose to 10 (from 6) and paused hit 19. Recurring gained net was only +1. A save conversation at cancel and a "we miss you" to paused members is what turns 37 into a climb.

4

Check-in coverage slid Improve

Attendance marking fell from 84% to 41% (excl. 12pm). The 35% attended / 65% no-show figure is less trustworthy until coaches mark every class again, including 12pm.
